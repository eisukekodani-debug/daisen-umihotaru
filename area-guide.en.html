Area Guide

Explore the rich nature and cuisine of Daisen Town

Beaches & Marine Activities

Kiryo Beach (木料海岸) — A Pristine Sandy Shore

Kiryo Beach (木料海岸), located about a 5-minute drive from DAISEN UMIHOTARU, is a beloved local swimming spot in Daisen Town. Featuring an unspoiled sandy shore, this uncommercialized natural beach offers a private beach-like atmosphere. During summer, the beach comes alive with families and visitors. Basic shower facilities are available for swimmers. The sunsets here are particularly stunning, as the sun sinks into the Sea of Japan. It is the perfect spot for a family trip or a group outing with friends.

Swimming Season: July - August

The most comfortable water temperatures of the year. Gentle waves make it ideal for families with small children.

Mikuriya Fishing Port (御来屋漁港) — Fresh Local Fish and Seafood

Located right next to DAISEN UMIHOTARU, Mikuriya Fishing Port (御来屋漁港) is an active fishing port where boats come and go daily. Inside the port, there is a fish market where you can purchase seafood freshly caught that very day. The selection ranges from premium sashimi-grade fish to small fish perfect for simmering. DAISEN UMIHOTARU also offers fishing gear for rent (paid), and boat fishing trips can be arranged. If you are interested, please contact us in advance so we can prepare everything for you.

At "Osakana Center Mikuriya" near the port, you can enjoy fresh seafood bowls and sashimi.

Recommended Season: Year-round

Seasonal fish varieties change throughout the year, and fresh seafood is available at the market all year long.

Mt. Daisen Nature & Outdoors

Daisen White Resort — A Four-Season Resort Experience

About a 25-minute drive from DAISEN UMIHOTARU, Daisen White Resort is one of Western Japan's premier ski resorts. During the winter ski and snowboard season, it offers high-quality slopes and well-equipped facilities suitable for beginners through advanced riders.

Winter: Skiing & Snowboarding (Late December - March)

Plenty of beginner-friendly ski schools available.

Daisenji Temple & Ogamiyama Shrine — History and Autumn Foliage

About a 25-minute drive from DAISEN UMIHOTARU, Daisenji Temple is an ancient Buddhist temple designated as a National Important Cultural Property. As the center of Daisen worship, it has been cherished by many pilgrims and reveals different expressions with each season. Ancient trees line the approach, creating a deeply atmospheric forest setting. The autumn foliage season (mid-October to mid-November) is especially breathtaking, with the mountainside ablaze in vivid reds and golds. Combined with a visit to Ogamiyama Shrine, it makes for a quintessential historical and cultural experience in Daisen.

Autumn Foliage: Mid-October to Mid-November

The most beautiful season when the approach is painted in autumn colors. Also popular with photography enthusiasts.

Daisen Makiba Milk no Sato — Farm Experiences and Gourmet Treats

About a 25-minute drive from DAISEN UMIHOTARU, Daisen Makiba Milk no Sato is a ranch spread across highland terrain at 900 meters above sea level. You can see free-range cattle up close. The rich, creamy soft-serve ice cream sold at the facility is beloved by many visitors. Cheese, butter, yogurt, and other dairy products are also available for purchase, making them popular souvenirs from Daisen. The location also offers stunning panoramic views of the Sea of Japan below.

Best Season: May - October

Enjoy the beauty of each season, from fresh greenery to autumn landscapes.

Daisen Beech Forest Walk — Nature Strolls and Hiking

For those who want to experience Daisen's nature up close, we recommend the Daisen Beech Forest Walk. This trail winds through a forest of beech trees over 300 years old, with routes ranging from beginner-friendly to advanced. The fresh greenery season (May - June) and autumn (September - October) are the best times for nature observation.

Wild vegetable foraging is also available in spring, offering the unique experience of harvesting mountain delicacies and cooking them on the spot. Breathing deeply in the heart of nature is the perfect way to refresh your mind and body from the stresses of city life.

Nawa Park (名和公園) — Cherry Blossoms Within Walking Distance

Nawa Park (名和公園), within walking distance from DAISEN UMIHOTARU, is a popular local spot for cherry blossom viewing. In spring, rows of cherry trees in full bloom line the pathways. With playground equipment and swings, it is also perfect for family strolls. Set on a hilltop with sea views, it is an ideal place to enjoy a relaxing time.

Cherry Blossom Season: Late March - Early April

A hidden gem bustling with local cherry blossom viewers. Playground equipment makes it great for families.

Nawa Shrine (名和神社) — A Historic Shrine and Cherry Blossoms

Nawa Shrine (名和神社), within walking distance from DAISEN UMIHOTARU, is a historic shrine dedicated to Nawa Nagatoshi, a warrior of the Nanbokucho period (14th century). The stone torii gate and cherry blossom-lined approach are beautiful, and spring brings a particularly elegant atmosphere. The tranquil shrine grounds are perfect for a leisurely stroll and offer a glimpse into local history.

The hillside paths around the shrine are also lined with cherry blossoms, perfect for a peaceful walk.

Hot Springs & Relaxation

Day-Trip Hot Springs Around Daisen

The area around Daisen offers several day-trip hot spring facilities, including Ravispa, Yume Onsen, Nakayama Onsen, and Goen Yuin. All are about a 15-minute drive from DAISEN UMIHOTARU and easily accessible. Options range from small, locally-loved baths to more fully-equipped spa facilities.

Gourmet & Dining

Partner Restaurant: Mikuri (御厨 Mikuri)

Mikuri (御厨 Mikuri), our partner restaurant, serves authentic cuisine crafted from fresh local ingredients from Daisen Town. Creative dishes made with locally grown vegetables, fresh fish, and high-quality meat are sure to delight every palate. Special course menus for groups are available with advance reservations.

Hawaiian Cafe BIG WAVE

Located right near DAISEN UMIHOTARU, Hawaiian Cafe BIG WAVE is a unique cafe housed in colorful containers. Enjoy a seaside atmosphere at this casual and fun spot to stop by.

Michi-no-Eki Daisen Megumi no Sato — Local Ingredients and Souvenirs

About a 5-minute drive from DAISEN UMIHOTARU, Michi-no-Eki "Daisen Megumi no Sato" is a roadside station offering a wide selection of local produce from Daisen Town. Fresh local vegetables, handmade seasonings, citrus fruits, and more are available, making it a convenient stop for BBQ ingredients and self-catering supplies.

In spring, bamboo shoots, bracken fern, and other wild vegetables line the shelves. A seasonal delight of Daisen's bounty.
