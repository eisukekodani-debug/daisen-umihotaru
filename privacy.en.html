Privacy Policy

DAISEN UMIHOTARU ("we") handles the information we receive through our official website (https://www.d-umihotaru.com) and through reservations and stays as described below.

1. Information we collect

  • When you book: your name, address, phone number, email address, number of guests and other details entered in the booking system
  • When you stay: the information required for the guest register under Japanese law
  • When you contact us: the content of your email
  • When you browse our website: browsing records collected with cookies and similar technologies (see section 3)

2. How we use it

  • To accept reservations, guide you during your stay and contact you
  • To keep the guest register required by law
  • To answer your inquiries
  • To send information about future stays to past guests
  • To improve our website

3. Analytics tools

Our website uses Google Analytics, provided by Google LLC, and Microsoft Clarity, provided by Microsoft Corporation. These tools use cookies to record the pages you view, the time you spend and your on-screen actions such as scrolling and clicking. These records do not include information that identifies you, such as your name.

  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. Booking system and payment

We use the AirHost booking system to accept reservations. Credit card information is processed by a payment service provider, and we do not keep your card number.

5. Disclosure to third parties

We do not provide your information to third parties except with your consent or where required by law.

6. Security

We manage your information appropriately to prevent leakage and loss.

7. Access, correction and deletion

If you wish to access, correct, stop the use of or delete your information, please contact us below. We will respond after confirming your identity.
