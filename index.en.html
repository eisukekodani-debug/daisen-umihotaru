The Story

An unreserved time,
Life by the sea etched in memory.

Mikuriya, Daisen Town, Tottori Prefecture.
In this port town, there stands a quiet house.
It is a private villa limited to one group per day.
Open the door, and the sea breeze and the sound of waves wash away the hustle and bustle of daily life.

A trip gathering with family and friends, a gentle time with loved ones.
In this spacious house that accommodates up to 14 people, even if children's laughter echoes, you don't need to worry about anyone.
Two staircases, maze-like rooms, and an ocean view filling the window.
Experience a journey not just to "stay," but to "live" in this town with your loved ones.

The open living room overlooking the sea is the center where everyone gathers. In the playroom on the 2nd floor, enjoy heated table tennis matches or play instruments. A space filled with smiles where you won't get bored even on rainy days.

A full kitchen where you can freely cook fresh ingredients from Daisen. The patio is also equipped with a BBQ grill. Spend irreplaceable time cooking and eating together.

From DAISEN UMIHOTARU to "Daisen White Resort" takes about 20-25 minutes by car.
