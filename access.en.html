Nearby Attractions

Kiryo Beach (木料海岸)
Approx. 5 min by car

A pristine sandy beach popular for summer swimming. Bustling with families during the season. Shower facilities available.

Daisen White Resort
Approx. 25 min by car

A winter paradise for skiing and snowboarding. Summer attractions include bobsled rides and family-friendly activities.

Daisenji Temple
Approx. 25 min by car

An ancient Buddhist temple with deep historical roots. Especially stunning during the autumn foliage season, attracting many visitors for worship and strolling.

Kaike Onsen
Approx. 25 min by car

A hot spring town overlooking the Sea of Japan. Numerous hotels and abundant hot spring water offer a soothing retreat.

Mikuriya Fishing Port (御来屋漁港)
Approx. 5 min on foot

An active fishing port right next door. Features a fresh fish market and eateries where you can savor local flavors.

Daisen Local Gourmet
Approx. 5-10 min by car

The Nawa area offers supermarkets, convenience stores, and home centers. Fresh local ingredients are also readily available.

Things to Enjoy by Season

  • Spring: Wild vegetable cuisine, strolling through fresh greenery
  • Summer: Swimming at Kiryo Beach, BBQ, hiking
  • Autumn: Autumn foliage viewing at Daisenji Temple, scenic drives
  • Winter: Skiing & snowboarding, hot springs, seasonal winter delicacies like matsuba crab

Before You Arrive

Winter Driving

During winter months (November - March), snow and icy road conditions may occur. We strongly recommend using snow tires or carrying tire chains. Extra caution is needed when driving toward Daisenji Temple.

Navigation Settings

Search for "824 Mikuriya" or coordinates "35.5091, 133.4915" on your car navigation system. When arriving, turn left at the final T-intersection to find our prominent building.

Parking Information

Free parking for 4 vehicles is available on the adjacent lot. If you need additional parking, please contact us in advance. We will provide guidance upon your arrival.

Arrival Time

Check-in starts at 4:00 PM. Please let us know in advance if your arrival time will differ significantly. If you arrive early, we recommend exploring the nearby attractions.
